# Ordinary merchant reagent references

41 explicitly selected common reagent IDs, captured 2026-11-11 from their
Wowhead Forever item sold-by tables. Only unlimited stock (-1), gold-only costs
and non-hostile NPC reactions are accepted. The minimum captured offer per
faction is divided by bundle stack count. Observed in-game unlimited buy prices
override reference numbers. Never derive a purchase price from vendor sell value.
NPC/reputation/location differences remain; these are labelled reference prices.
No promise that every new reagent or every merchant is covered. Limited stock,
recipe books and currency exchanges are not generalised as unlimited supplies.
Numeric offer facts only; no guide prose or third-party implementation is copied.
