# CraftDeja

**Craft smarter. Spend less. Level your professions.**

CraftDeja is a price-aware profession adviser for **WoW Forever**, available in **English and German**. Compare estimated material costs per skill point, see what to craft next and get a clear next-step checklist.

## Features

- **Smart planning:** recommendations across 2,508 reference recipes and nine crafting areas, with faction-aware sources, skill-color ranges and Wowhead links.
- **Shop to the next action:** plan materials until a recipe change, learning step or rank cap. A manual 1–50 skill-point slider is also available.
- **Auctionator integration:** automatic full scans when needed; prices remain valid for 30 minutes. Shopping stays locked until the current scan completes. Planned quantities and price limits are checked before purchases.
- **Clear next steps:** collect confirmed AH purchases, visit a reagent vendor, retrieve bank materials and prepare crafts. Outstanding mail items appear together; important tasks are highlighted.
- **Vendor savings:** buy ordinary supplies from vendors when cheaper, with a vendor purchase popup. Supplier names, coordinates and CraftDeja map markers help you find them.
- **Inventory overview:** item tooltips show bags, banks and recorded alt stock. Log in each alt and open its bank to update saved counts.
- **Trainer guidance:** matching rank trainers, map markers and prominent character-level requirements. Eligible missing recipes appear as learning recommendations.
- **Recipe change reports:** detects unknown recipes, ingredient differences and difficulty discrepancies from your profession window. View the data-package date and copy unverified observations with `/cdj data`.
- **Compact Goblin UI:** item icons, highlighted native crafting buttons, an in-game guide, drag-to-scale windows and a diagnostic log.

## Quick start

1. Open your profession so CraftDeja can read your skill and learned recipes.
2. Open the AH and let Auctionator finish its scan. Keep it open while scanning.
3. Follow **Next step**: review and confirm purchases, collect mail, buy vendor supplies and craft.

**You confirm every purchase. Crafting is started with your click in the native profession window.** Automatic scans respect AH cooldowns and active purchases.

Commands: `/cdj` · `/cdj help` · `/cdj shopping` · `/cdj log` · `/cdj data`

**Beta:** skill-up probabilities, quantities and some source/trainer locations use reference estimates. Prices and saved inventories can change; Auctionator compatibility depends on its version.

## Help test CraftDeja

**Found a bug? Please report it in the CurseForge comments!** Reproduce the issue, open `/cdj log`, copy the log with **Ctrl+C** and paste it into your comment. Include your **CraftDeja version**, profession and a short description of what happened. For recipe differences, also include the report from `/cdj data`. Screenshots are welcome. Your reports help improve the addon!
