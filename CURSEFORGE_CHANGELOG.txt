## CraftDeja 3.7.0 Beta 1
**Recipe observations and visible data status**

- Detect unknown recipes, complete ingredient differences and difficulty discrepancies when opening your profession.
- View the bundled data-package date and report count in the adviser.
- Open **Recipe check** or use `/cdj data` to copy the observation report.
- Keep reports across sessions, merge duplicates and limit storage to 200 observations per character.
- Ignore incomplete ingredient data; do not automatically change the shared reference catalogue.
- Include up to 20 recent recipe observations in `/cdj log`.

### Help test CraftDeja

Please post bugs in the CurseForge comments with your version, profession and steps to reproduce. Copy `/cdj log` with **Ctrl+C**; for recipe differences, also copy `/cdj data`.

Reports are **unverified observations**, not confirmed recipe changes. No automatic report upload takes place. The package date identifies the snapshot and does not mean every recipe was revalidated on that date.

Requires Auctionator or TSM price data; AH shopping requires Auctionator. All existing shopping, mail, vendor and trainer features remain included.

**Beta:** the new recipe-observation workflow still needs live in-game testing.
